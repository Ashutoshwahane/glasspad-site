Two minutes, once

Install and pair.

  1. 1

    Open the download.

    Unzip it, move GlassPad Mac to your Applications folder, then open it.

  2. 2

    Allow Accessibility.

    When asked, turn on GlassPad Mac in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility (on macOS 27: Device Control and Data Access), so it can move the pointer and type for you.

  3. 3

    Scan to pair.

    On the same Wi-Fi, open GlassPad on your iPhone or iPad, tap Connect, and scan the QR code shown on your Mac.
