Help when you need it

A simple connection.

The iPhone/iPad app is currently in testing. The free Mac companion 1.0 (4) is available for macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon and Intel).

Download GlassPad Mac 1.0 (4)

Unzip the download, move GlassPad Mac.app to Applications, then open it. The companion is signed and notarized. Allow Accessibility access when prompted. Download verification: release notes and SHA-256 checksum.

Connect to your Mac

  1. Open GlassPad Mac and allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  2. Keep your iPhone or iPad and Mac on the same Wi-Fi network. Resume control if the Mac companion is paused.
  3. On your device, open GlassPad → Connect → Scan Mac QR code. Alternatively, copy the Mac pairing code and paste it in the app, then tap Pair and Connect.
  4. Allow Local Network access. Camera access is needed only for scanning; you can use a pasted code instead.

Cursor or gestures not responding?

Check the access status first: all remote controls require an active trial or verified lifetime purchase. In Keyboard → Quick shortcuts, Copy, Paste, Undo and Select All send Command-C/V/Z/A to the focused Mac application. Select text before Copy and focus a text field before Paste.

Check that the companion is running, control is resumed, and Accessibility permission is enabled. Guest Wi-Fi and network isolation can block device-to-device connections. Reconnect after returning to the app, or pair again after quitting either app.

Use one finger to move and tap, two to scroll or right-click. Tap Drag to hold a selection and Release to let go. Three-finger navigation requires the corresponding Control + arrow shortcuts in macOS Keyboard settings.

Full-app trial and lifetime access

After your first successful Mac connection, close the Connect sheet and choose Start 7-day full-app trial in Unlock GlassPad. Explicitly confirm Apple’s free 7-day Trial. All remote controls unlock for seven days from that verified original transaction date. Connection alone does not start the clock. After expiry, pointer, clicks, scrolling, drag, keyboard, text and shortcuts lock until a one-time lifetime purchase. No automatic charge or renewal. Prices appear in the app before confirmation. Pairing, settings, help and Restore Purchases remain accessible.

Use Restore Purchases in Unlock GlassPad with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Restore on a reinstalled app or another device keeps the original trial end date; an expired trial stays expired. Lifetime access restores without another payment. Apple handles billing and refund requests.
