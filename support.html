Help when you need it

A simple connection.

GlassPad is currently in testing. Public app and Mac companion downloads are not available yet.

Connect to your Mac

  1. Open GlassPad Mac and allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  2. Keep your iPhone or iPad and Mac on the same Wi-Fi network. Resume control if the Mac companion is paused.
  3. On your device, open GlassPad → Connect → Scan Mac QR code. Alternatively, copy the Mac pairing code and paste it in the app, then tap Pair and Connect.
  4. Allow Local Network access. Camera access is needed only for scanning; you can use a pasted code instead.

Cursor or gestures not responding?

Check that the companion is running, control is resumed, and Accessibility permission is enabled. Guest Wi-Fi and network isolation can block device-to-device connections. Reconnect after returning to the app, or pair again after quitting either app.

Use one finger to move and tap, two to scroll or right-click. Tap Drag to hold a selection and Release to let go. Pro three-finger navigation requires the corresponding Control + arrow shortcuts in macOS Keyboard settings.

Pro, trial and purchases

Basic controls remain free. The optional 7-day Pro trial unlocks Pro temporarily and never renews or charges automatically. After it ends, Pro controls lock unless you purchase the lifetime unlock. Prices appear in the app before confirmation.

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase. A trial does not restart when restored. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Get help or report a problem

Contact support on GitHub

Include your device model, OS version, app version, and the steps that caused the issue. GitHub Issues are public: do not include pairing codes, passwords, identity documents, payment details, or other private information. Do not attach screenshots containing those details.
